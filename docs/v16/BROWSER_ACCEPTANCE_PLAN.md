# V16 bounded native acceptance plan

Use the exact published candidate only after the parent schedules a controlled browser window. Preserve the user's save, owned car, balance and settings; do not purchase, top up or reset progress. This test cannot earn coins or alter progression.

1. Confirm Help says V16; from Home open Help → Solo surface test. Start the already-owned car. Confirm SOLO / NO REWARD, disabled race choices and no boost/flight controls.
2. Accelerate down the level approach and hold a clear centerline through the bank, vertical hold and return. Observe real wheel/car orientation and camera-up blending, road stripe/texture continuity, and no scenery penetration. A little steering changes lateral position on the wall, not only body roll.
3. On the wall, pause: car, camera, time and audio mix must stop. Resume, then brake to low speed and observe contact release followed by safe recovery. Test manual R at a safe time. Do not interpret the intended finite-adhesion release as a crash simulation.
4. Reach the final level runout; confirm endpoint warning. Recover, turn around or drive back. Test reverse on the flat approach. Confirm no coin/Story/boost reward and unchanged balance.
5. Check chase, bonnet and high camera; one desktop and compact/portrait sample. Record visible clipping, motion discomfort, FPS/draw/triangle point samples and whether touch controls remain usable. Do not call point samples a sustained benchmark.
6. Return to a normal course through its button. Briefly confirm normal drive/boost/camera and saved ownership remain intact. Stop inputs and return the original viewport/settings.

Prior V15 natural feature checks, if time remains: Explore → Divided Highway then ~300 m to its ramp; Coast Explore → Skyline Flyover then ~145 m to its ramp. Aircraft are absent in Explore; police air support must be reached naturally in Hot Pursuit. No debug teleport, forced heat or save manipulation.

Known limits: no full loop/ceiling/tube, no AI on the proof, finite adhesion, conservative safety return rather than structure collision impulses, decorative outer rails, no GPU result assumed from Node/Blender tests. Small aircraft silhouette and the earlier V15 Highland 41.9 FPS point remain separate visibility/performance risks, not proven V16 regressions.
