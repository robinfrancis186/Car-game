Help & settings

MALABAR V17 · Arcade racing prototype

Manual only on Harbour Wall Run. Ground-road Auto Drive cannot control the banked adhesion surface or guarantee the speed needed to stay attached.

Switch with T or the driving toggle. Auto Drive follows the current road; tap arrows or swipe to choose left, center or right lines. Hold BRAKE or Space to stop; release to resume. Boost stays under your control. No new junctions or collision immunity. Each new run starts Manual.

Resolution comparison lasts this session only; it does not change car detail or shadows. Performance readings settle for five active seconds after a viewport, camera, quality or resolution change. Capturing screenshots can affect measurements.

Auto Drive (T) follows the road and manages pedals. Tap left/right for a driving line, or down for center; hold BRAKE/Space to override. Manual keeps full steering and pedals. Optional Pedals-only assistance is in Help. New runs start Manual; the experimental wall test remains Manual. On a phone in manual mode, hold GO to accelerate, the arrows to steer, BRAKE to slow down and REV to reverse. Landscape gives you a wider view. Pass every pink gate in order. If you miss one, follow the radar back or recover with R. The Kappil-derived route is remixed with fictional Kerala districts. Flight is an arcade mechanic, not a production-car simulation.

Racing, coins & upgrades

COINS: Crimson and Silver are free. Finish races to bank the finish award and any collected race coins. Failed or abandoned runs and Explore do not bank coins. Locked cars show their coin price in the Shop. All coins are local game currency, never real money. Existing saves keep their balance; new profiles start at zero.

STORY: Three original coastal chapters use the same race rules and coin awards as quick races. Complete each objective to unlock the next chapter. Camera intros can be skipped with Enter, Space or SKIP; Escape returns to the briefing. Story progress is saved separately from your garage.

RAMPS: Marked ramps launch from the road without flight controls. A clean landing can refill some boost once per ramp per run; the HUD reports the amount actually added. Manual flight, flips, collisions and recovery do not earn this bonus. For the Highland ramp, choose Explore → Divided Highway, then drive about 300 m. For the coastal ramp, choose Explore → Skyline Flyover, then drive about 145 m. Police air support joins sustained high-heat pursuits with an obstruction-aware searchlight; it cannot arrest or damage you by itself.

BRAKING upgrades strengthen the service brake (Space or BRAKE), not reverse motor braking. Calipers stay red at levels 0–1, become orange at level 2 and violet at level 3. Handling upgrades add the visible aero trim; they are not a second purchase.

Solo surface test

Original optional wall-run proof: a smooth bank reaches 90° and returns to level. Keep moving on the wall; low speed or crossing the edge releases contact. R returns to the flat approach. Manual controls only: the ground-road Auto Drive controller is not designed for this 3D banked adhesion surface. No coins, boost rewards, thruster flight, police or opponents in this test. Falling into support piers or signs triggers a safe return, not a simulated crash. Outer rails are visual guidance; crossing the road edge releases contact. Start from Home; use the normal course buttons to return to Kerala races.
